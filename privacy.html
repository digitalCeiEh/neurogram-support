Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 12, 2026

In short: Your thoughts are stored on your device. When you interact with the AI, your text is routed through our secure infrastructure (Cloudflare) and processed by OpenAI's API. Neither Cloudflare nor OpenAI retains your data or uses it to train their models. We can't read your entries.

1. Who We Are

Neurogram is a personal AI journaling application developed by an independent developer based in Italy. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

For any questions about this policy, contact us at neurogram2026@gmail.com.

2. Data We Collect

Neurogram is designed with a privacy-first architecture. Here is what we collect and what we don't:

Data stored locally on your device only:

  • Your written thoughts and voice transcriptions
  • AI-generated insights and analysis
  • Conversation history with the AI assistant
  • AI memory (what Neurogram has learned about you)
  • App preferences and settings

Data we do NOT collect:

  • We do not create user accounts
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, or personal identifiers
  • We do not track your location
  • We do not use analytics or tracking SDKs
  • We do not store any of your data on our servers

3. How AI Processing Works

When you use Neurogram's AI features (insights, conversation, analysis), your text is routed through our secure proxy infrastructure hosted on Cloudflare and then sent to OpenAI's API for processing. This is necessary to generate personalized responses.

Important details about this processing:

  • Your data passes through Cloudflare's network in transit but is not stored or logged by Cloudflare
  • Data sent to OpenAI's API is not used to train their models, in accordance with OpenAI's API data usage policy
  • OpenAI may temporarily process the data to generate a response, after which it is not retained for training purposes
  • We send only the text needed to generate a response — no device identifiers or personal metadata
  • You can review OpenAI's API data usage policy at openai.com/policies/api-data-usage-policies

4. Voice Input

If you use voice input, your audio is routed through our secure proxy infrastructure and processed by OpenAI's Whisper API to convert speech to text. The audio is used for transcription only and is not stored by us, Cloudflare, or OpenAI, and is not used for model training. The resulting text is then stored locally on your device like any other entry.

5. Data Storage & Security

All your personal data (thoughts, insights, AI memory, conversations) is stored exclusively on your device's local storage. We do not operate servers that store user content. All data transmitted between the app and our infrastructure is encrypted using TLS.

This means:

  • If you delete the app, all your data is permanently deleted
  • If you lose your device, your data cannot be recovered
  • No one — including us — can access your data remotely

6. Subscription & Payments

Neurogram uses RevenueCat to manage subscriptions. When you subscribe, RevenueCat processes your purchase status through Apple's App Store or Google Play. RevenueCat does not have access to your thoughts, entries, AI memory, or any personal content within the app. Payment information is handled entirely by Apple or Google — we never see your credit card details.

7. Your Rights & Control

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • View your data: All your entries and AI memory are visible directly in the app
  • Export your data: Use the "Export My Data" feature in Settings to download your data
  • Delete specific memories: Use "Clear Memory" to erase what the AI has learned about you
  • Delete all data: Use "Erase All Data" in Settings to permanently remove everything, or simply delete the app

8. Children's Privacy

Neurogram is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the app, deleting the app will remove all locally stored data.

9. Third-Party Services

Neurogram uses the following third-party services:

  • OpenAI API — for AI text generation and voice transcription. Their privacy policy is available at openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
  • Cloudflare — for secure request routing and infrastructure protection. Your data passes through Cloudflare's network in transit but is not stored or logged. Their privacy policy is available at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy
  • RevenueCat — for subscription management. RevenueCat processes your purchase status through Apple/Google but does not have access to your thoughts, entries, or AI data. Their privacy policy is available at revenuecat.com/privacy

We do not use advertising networks, analytics services, crash reporting tools, or any other third-party SDKs that collect user data.

10. Changes to This Policy
