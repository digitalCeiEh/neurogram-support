Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Your thoughts are yours

Neurogram is designed to protect your privacy. Your thoughts and personal data are treated with the utmost confidentiality.

What data we collect

We only collect the data necessary to make the app work:

• Account: name and password to identify you

• Thoughts: the content you write in the app

• Usage data: anonymous statistics to improve the app

How we use your data

Your thoughts are processed to generate personalized insights and allow the AI to understand you better.

Third-party services

To provide artificial intelligence features, Neurogram uses OpenAI services. When you write thoughts and interact with the AI, your content is sent to OpenAI servers for processing.

OpenAI processes this data according to its own Privacy Policy. We use OpenAI's APIs with the option to not use data for model training.

We do not sell or share your data with third parties for advertising purposes.

Security

Your data is protected with encryption and stored on secure servers. Only you can access your thoughts.

Your rights

You can at any time:

• Export all your data

• Request the deletion of your account

• Contact us with any questions
